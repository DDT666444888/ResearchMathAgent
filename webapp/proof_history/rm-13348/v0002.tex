\section*{Why no proof can be produced}

A proof is a finite argument that establishes the truth of a precise
proposition $P$ from stated axioms or hypotheses. Such an argument exists
only once $P$ is fixed. Here no proposition $P$ is present: the input is a
prose passage about human communication, and the accompanying remark merely
\emph{states} that no mathematical content is available to formalize. Hence
there is no theorem, lemma, or claim toward which a derivation could be
directed.

Manufacturing ``mathematical content'' would require inventing a statement
absent from the source. That would be both dishonest and mathematically
meaningless: any such fabricated statement, and any ``proof'' of it, would
bear no relation to the entry rm-13348. The intellectually honest outcome is
therefore to record that the task is vacuous.

\section*{Conclusion}

The entry rm-13348 is non-mathematical. No well-posed proof task can be
extracted from it, and consequently none can be carried out. This document
records that determination as the final, correct resolution of the entry.
