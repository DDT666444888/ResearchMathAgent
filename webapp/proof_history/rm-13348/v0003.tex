\section*{Why no proof can be produced}

A proof is a finite chain of inferences that establishes the truth of a
precise proposition $P$ from stated axioms or hypotheses. Such a chain can
exist only after $P$ has been fixed. Here no proposition $P$ is present: the
underlying input is a prose passage about human communication, and the
accompanying remark merely \emph{states} that no mathematical content is
available to formalize. Consequently there is no theorem, lemma, or claim
toward which any derivation could be directed, and the notion of
``correctness'' of a proof has no referent.

Manufacturing ``mathematical content'' would require inventing a statement
absent from the source. Any such fabricated statement, and any ``proof'' of
it, would bear no relation to the entry rm-13348 and would be mathematically
meaningless as a resolution of it. The intellectually honest outcome is
therefore to record that the task is vacuous.
