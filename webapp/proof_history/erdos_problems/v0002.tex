\section*{Why no proof is written}

Erd\H{o}s problems are extremely sensitive to phrasing (``for all large
$n$'' versus ``infinitely often''; exact constants versus asymptotics
$O,o,\sim,\asymp$; and open versus solved versus disproved status), and
the dataset contains $1217$ candidate problems. With no verbatim
statement, hypotheses $H$, conclusion $C$, or logical form, there is
literally nothing to assess: standard proof-gap taxonomy (definitional
drift, boundary cases, unjustified steps, quantifier-order errors) is
inapplicable because no claim exists. Producing any ``proof'' would
require fabricating both the claim and its argument, which violates
grounding and faithfulness and would, with overwhelming probability, prove
the wrong statement. A fabricated proof is mathematically worthless and
actively harmful (it would falsely signal a completed result).
